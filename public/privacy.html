Privacy Policy

Cram turns photos and documents of study material into flashcards. This page explains what the app does with your data. It is short because the app collects very little.

What we process

  • Photos, screenshots and PDFs you choose to scan. When you tap the shutter or import a file, that image or document is sent to our server and then to Anthropic's Claude API to generate flashcards. It is used only to generate your cards, is not stored by our server after the response is returned, and is subject to Anthropic's commercial terms, under which inputs are not used to train models.
  • Your decks and study progress. Stored only on your device. We do not have a copy. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Usage counters. The number of cards and scans you have made today is stored on your device to apply the free tier. It is not sent anywhere.

What we do not collect

  • No account, email address, name or password. Cram does not have sign-in.
  • No location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive an anonymous receipt to confirm your subscription is active. We never see your payment details.

Camera and photo permissions

Cram asks for camera access to scan pages and photo-library access only when you choose to import an existing photo. Both can be revoked in Settings; the app continues to work with whatever you have allowed.

Children

Cram is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will change and the app's store listing will be updated.

Contact

Use the support link on Cram's App Store listing.
